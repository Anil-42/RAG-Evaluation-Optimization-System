The reranker isn't optimizing your exact evaluation metric.

It is trying to determine:
"Which chunk is most relevant to this question?"

Your evaluation is asking:
"How much of the required evidence did the retrieved chunks contain?"

Those aren't necessarily the same thing.
For example, you found that the Hybrid system retrieved a chunk containing all nine evidence points for one question, but the cross-encoder assigned that chunk a relatively poor score and removed it.

That's an excellent concrete example to include in your eventual project report.
